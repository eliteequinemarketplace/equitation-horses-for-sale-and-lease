Big Eq Horses for Sale — ASPCA Maclay, USEF Medal & Talent Search

The three flagship Big Eq finals define the pinnacle of junior equitation competition in the United States — and the horses that compete at this level are among the most talented and sought-after in the sport.

ASPCA Maclay: Established in 1933 and held at the National Horse Show each November, the NHS ASPCA Maclay National Championship is one of the most prestigious junior competitions in the country. Courses are set at 3'6" and judged on seat, hands, guidance, and control of the horse. The Maclay Final includes an initial round over fences, a flat phase, a second round over fences, and further testing. A Maclay-quality horse must have exceptional flatwork, a correct and flowing jump at 3'6", and the trainability to perform advanced tests under pressure. In 2025, 176 riders competed in the Maclay Final.

USEF Hunter Seat Medal: Held at the Pennsylvania National Horse Show, the USEF Hunter Seat Medal Final is one of the Big Three eq finals, with courses at 3'6". The ideal Medal horse combines correct hunter movement with the scope and adjustability to perform advanced flat and jumping tests at the national level.

Platinum Performance/USEF Show Jumping Talent Search — "Gladstone": The crown jewel of junior equitation, the USEF Talent Search is open to riders 21 and under and is widely known as "Gladstone" after the Talent Search Finals East location at the USET Foundation Headquarters in Gladstone, NJ. The Finals West is held at the Desert International Horse Park in Thermal, California. The Talent Search features a four-phase format — flat, gymnastics, a jumper-style course, and a final ride-off where the top four athletes swap horses — making it the most demanding equitation final in the country. Courses are larger than the Medal and Maclay and include Liverpools, open water, and triple combinations. A Talent Search horse must have genuine show jumping scope, bravery, and the consistent trainability to perform for multiple riders in the ride-off. These horses represent the absolute top end of the equitation market.

WIHS Equitation Classic: Held at the Washington International Horse Show (CSI5*-W), the WIHS Equitation Classic features both a hunter phase and a jumper phase — one of the most unique and demanding equitation finals in the country. Horses competing at WIHS must be versatile, brave, and consistently correct across two very different phases.

USHJA Gladstone Cup Equitation Classic: A prestigious three-round equitation class held in conjunction with the USEF Junior Hunter National Championships, the Gladstone Cup combines equitation precision with hunter handiness over a course set at 3'6". To qualify, riders must have won a USEF Hunter Seat Medal, ASPCA Maclay, USEF Show Jumping Talent Search, or either phase of the WIHS Equitation Classic. Finals are held bi-coastally — East and West — making it one of the most selective equitation invitational classes in the sport.

WEC Premier Equitation Cup Championship: One of the most prestigious and exciting equitation championships of the year, the WEC Premier Equitation Cup is held at World Equestrian Center in Ocala, Florida. The competition features a traditional three-phase format with the possibility of a work-off, a $100,000 trainer bonus, and 75 of the nation's top junior equitation athletes competing for the championship title. In 2025, the winner also received the keys to a brand-new car — making it one of the highest-profile equitation events outside the traditional Big Eq calendar.

3'3" Medal Horses — Stepping Stones to Big Eq

For junior riders developing toward the Big Eq, the 3'3" medal programs provide essential stepping stones — and horses that excel at this level are in high demand.

Hamel Foundation NHS 3'3" Medal: Open to junior riders who have never competed in a 3'6" national final, the NHS 3'3" Medal is one of the most prestigious stepping-stone equitation finals in the country. Held at the National Horse Show, it is restricted to riders who have not yet advanced to the Big Eq level — making a qualified NHS 3'3" horse a valued partner for developing juniors.

Marshall+Sterling/USHJA 3'3" Jumping Seat Medal: A three-phase final featuring flat, gymnastics, and jumping phases at 3'3", the Jumping Seat Medal develops riders toward the USEF Talent Search. Finals are held bi-coastally at the Capital Challenge Horse Show (East) and on the West Coast.

IHSA/USHJA 3'3" Hunter Seat Medal: A stepping stone for junior riders, with finals held bi-coastally each fall. Horses must earn a minimum of 10 qualifying points to advance to the finals.

Junior Equitation Horses for Sale

Junior equitation horses are some of the most carefully selected horses in the sport. Unlike hunters — where the horse is judged on its own way of going — equitation judges score the rider, meaning the horse must be a reliable, consistent, and educated partner that allows the rider to focus entirely on their own position and aids. The ideal junior equitation horse has correct, rhythmic flatwork, a balanced and adjustable canter, the scope to jump confidently at the required height, and the patience to perform tests and work-offs without tension.

Equitation Schoolmaster Horses for Sale

An equitation schoolmaster is one of the most valuable horses in the sport — an experienced, educated horse that actively teaches its rider rather than requiring management. The best equitation schoolmasters are honest, consistent, and genuinely rideable: they accept the leg, respond softly to the hand, maintain a balanced canter, and produce a correct, comfortable jump that allows riders to focus entirely on their own position. Elite Equine Marketplace regularly features equitation schoolmasters from trusted trainers worldwide — horses that have competed successfully at every level from local medals through national finals.

Adult Equitation Horses for Sale — Ariat Medal & THIS Medal

Adult equitation is one of the fastest-growing divisions in the sport, and the demand for quality adult equitation horses has never been higher. The two primary adult equitation finals are the Ariat National Adult Medal Finals and the Taylor Harris Insurance Services (THIS) National Children's Medal adult sections — both held at the Capital Challenge Horse Show. Adult equitation horses must be safe, pleasant, and consistent for non-professional adult riders, with correct movement and a reliable jump that allows adult amateurs to compete confidently in the equitation ring.

Young Equitation Prospects for Sale

Young equitation prospects are typically 4 to 7 year old warmbloods or sport horses with the natural qualities that equitation demands — correct, flowing flatwork, a natural balance and rhythm at all three gaits, a comfortable and adjustable canter, and genuine scope to jump well at 3'3" and above. The best equitation prospects combine the hunter horse's beauty and way of going with the scope and trainability of a show jumper. Elite Equine Marketplace lists young equitation prospects from reputable breeders and trainers worldwide.
